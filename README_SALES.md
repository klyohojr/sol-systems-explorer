# SOL Systems Explorer — live sales stack

## Product ladder
- Lite: $29 — simple 2–4 module explainers
- Starter: $79 — general-purpose kit
- Studio: $199 — reusable client-project workflow
- Concept Explorer Pilot: $2,500–$5,000 planning range
- Technical System Explorer: $10,000–$30,000 planning range

## Sales doctrine
- Use the smallest offer that solves the prospect's problem.
- Self-serve outreach uses an offer CTA and direct product URL rather than asking for a meeting.
- Keep cold outreach short, specific, and connected to the recipient's actual workflow.
- Do not invent customers, ROI, scarcity, integrations, engineering validation, or unsupported capabilities.
- Follow up by adding value, not with generic 'checking in' messages.

## Fulfillment
Stripe live payment links are monitored by the SOL Product Fulfillment automation. Paid sessions are delivered from private Drive artifacts and deduplicated through Gmail sent-message search.
