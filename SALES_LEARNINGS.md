# SOL Systems Explorer — Outreach Learnings

## 2026-09-11: Lucent / Dominik Schmidt

### Evidence
- Initial cold pitch led with Studio ($199), a long capability list, checkout, and overview.
- Dominik replied and asked for examples: a genuine curiosity signal, but not yet a buying signal.
- We answered with three unrelated public-story examples (Jaipur Robotics, Harmoni/HAL, CloudNC).
- We then researched Lucent and built a much more relevant Lucent × Slamcore concept, but only after the examples response had already been sent.
- After seeing the tailored concept, Dominik explicitly declined: “no interest thanks.” Treat Lucent as closed; no further outreach unless they initiate.

### Guardrails learned
1. Do prospect research and prepare the best relevant proof **before** replying to an examples request.
2. An examples request means curiosity, not purchase intent. Do not mentally promote it to a near-sale.
3. Prefer one highly analogous/tailored example over three loosely related examples.
4. Cold first touches should lead with the prospect's communication problem/outcome; avoid opening with price + feature inventory.
5. Do not include checkout in the first cold email unless the prospect explicitly asks how to buy or the context is strongly transactional.
6. After curiosity appears, answer exactly what was asked. Only introduce the smallest offer when the prospect indicates a use case, scope, price question, or desire to proceed.
7. Tailored speculative work has a cost. Build it only when expected learning/value justifies it; do not overinvest after a weak signal.
8. A clear “no interest” closes the prospect. Record the lesson; do not rebut, nurture, or follow up.
9. Judge outreach by funnel stages separately: delivered → reply → curiosity → qualified need → offer → checkout → paid. Do not conflate reply rate with buying intent.
10. Delivery failures are a separate lane: never resend to a known bad address; find a verified alternate channel only when justified.
