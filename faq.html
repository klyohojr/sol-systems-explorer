SOL Systems Explorer FAQ

What is SOL Systems Explorer Starter Kit?

A $79 one-time digital template for creating browser-based interactive technical system explainers without a build pipeline.

What can I edit?

The included workflow lets you change the system title, module names, descriptions, roles, statuses, and theme through editable JSON and then export a customized single-file HTML explainer.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The viewer is designed for mobile-friendly navigation as well as desktop browsers.

Do I need CAD or a JavaScript build system?

No. The Starter Kit is self-contained. It also is not CAD software and does not replace engineering authoring tools.

What if I want the explainer built for me?

Choose Explorer Quickstart. It is a $499 fixed-scope done-for-you build for one bounded system with up to 8 modules, customer branding, mobile + desktop delivery, standalone HTML, and one revision. The target is 3 business days after complete approved materials are received.

Which edition should I buy?

Lite is $29 for simple 2–4 module explainers. Starter is $79 for general use up to 6 modules. Studio is $199 for repeated client work and up to 12 modules. Quickstart is $499 when you want us to build the bounded explainer for you. Larger bespoke work moves into a custom pilot.

Can I use exported explainers commercially?

Yes, for the purchaser or purchasing organization, subject to the product terms. The original Starter Kit package may not be redistributed as a competing template.

Is it a digital twin or engineering simulation?

No. It is a visualization and technical-communication template, not engineering simulation, certified digital-twin software, or safety-critical engineering validation.

What support is included?

Reasonable support for file-access problems and reproducible defects in the supplied Starter Kit. Custom implementation and consulting are separate services.

What is the refund policy?

See the Refund Policy. It covers cases such as duplicate purchase, access failure after troubleshooting, or a material defect we cannot reasonably fix or replace, subject to applicable law.
