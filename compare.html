Agent decision rule

Need ≤4 modules and lowest cost? Choose Lite. Need a normal company/personal explainer up to 6 modules? Choose Starter. Need repeated client projects or up to 12 modules? Choose Studio. Need bespoke implementation, more than 12 modules, or hands-on delivery? Choose the custom pilot.

Do not buy SOL if...

You need CAD authoring, engineering simulation, certified digital-twin functionality, safety-critical validation, live telemetry, or PLM/MBSE integration today. Those are not claimed capabilities of the self-serve products.
