Last updated: September 9, 2026.

Self-serve digital products

SOL Explorer Lite, Starter Kit, and Studio Pack are digital products delivered electronically after successful payment. Refund requests should be made within 14 days of purchase.

We will generally consider refunds for duplicate purchases, persistent file-access failures after reasonable troubleshooting, or a material defect that prevents a core advertised function when we cannot provide a reasonable fix or replacement.

Change-of-mind requests after successful access to the digital files are generally not refundable. Likewise, incompatibility with a workflow, platform, integration, or feature that was not advertised as supported is generally not grounds for a refund.

Quickstart service

For SOL Explorer Quickstart, contact us promptly if you need to cancel. Before substantive work begins, we will generally consider cancellation and refund requests. After substantive work has begun, any refund may reflect work already performed, except where applicable law or a material failure to provide the purchased service requires otherwise.

How to request help or a refund

Reply to the purchase, delivery, or project email and include the email used at checkout plus the order or Checkout Session reference when available. For a defect, include enough information to reproduce the issue.

Nothing in this policy limits any non-waivable consumer rights or remedies that apply under applicable law.
