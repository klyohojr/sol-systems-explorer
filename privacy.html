Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 8, 2026.

Public product site

The public SOL Systems Explorer product page is a static informational site. The Starter Kit itself runs in the buyer's browser and does not intentionally transmit the buyer's edited system JSON to SOL.

Purchases and fulfillment

When you purchase, the payment provider processes payment and checkout information under its own privacy terms. We receive the transaction information needed to verify the purchase and deliver the digital product, such as the checkout email, payment status, and order/session reference.

Digital fulfillment may use connected email and cloud-storage services to send the purchased file. Those providers process data according to their own terms and privacy practices.

Shopify and agentic discovery

If a purchase or product discovery occurs through Shopify or an AI commerce channel connected through Shopify, Shopify and the relevant channel may process product, account, browsing, and transaction information under their applicable policies.

Support communications

If you contact us or reply to a product-delivery email, we may retain that communication as reasonably needed to provide support, prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve disputes, and maintain business records.

Changes

If we later add hosted accounts, analytics, telemetry, cloud saves, or API services that collect additional personal data, this notice will be updated before those features are launched.
